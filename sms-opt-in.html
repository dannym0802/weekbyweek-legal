SMS opt-in method

Proof of how end users consent to receive SMS from Danny Mauro / Week by Week.

How users opt in

Opt-in happens inside the Week by Week iOS/Android app (not via a pre-checked box or purchased list):

  1. The user opens Week by Week and sees the Sign in screen.
  2. They enter their mobile phone number.
  3. They tap Send code, which requests an SMS one-time passcode for authentication and constitutes consent to receive those SMS messages.
  4. They enter the code from the SMS to complete sign-in.

Supporting disclosures are available publicly: Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions (including SMS Terms).

Opt out

Users can reply STOP to SMS messages, reply HELP for help, email dannymauro0802@gmail.com, or delete their account in Settings.
