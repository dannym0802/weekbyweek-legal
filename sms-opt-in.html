SMS opt-in method

Proof of how end users consent to receive SMS from Week by Week.

Message flow (call to action)

End users opt in only inside the Week by Week iOS/Android app (not via keyword text-in, purchased lists, or a pre-checked website box):

  1. The user opens Week by Week and goes to the Sign in screen.
  2. They choose the Phone sign-in option.
  3. They enter their mobile phone number.
  4. Below the phone field they see the required SMS consent disclosures (program name, message type, frequency, rates, STOP/HELP, and links to Privacy Policy and Terms).
  5. They tap Send code, which is affirmative consent to receive recurring automated SMS messages from Week by Week for account authentication (one-time passcodes).
  6. They enter the code from the SMS to complete sign-in.

Required consent language (shown at opt-in)

By tapping Send code, you agree to receive recurring automated text messages from Week by Week for account authentication (one-time passcodes / login verification). Message frequency varies (typically one message each time you request a sign-in code). Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase. See our Privacy Policy and Terms.

Send code

After tapping Send code, the user receives an SMS one-time passcode used only for login verification.

Message type & sample

Transactional authentication only (no marketing or promotional blasts).

Sample SMS 1:
Week by Week: Your login code is 123456. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.

Sample SMS 2:
Week by Week: your verification code is 123456. Do not share this code. Reply STOP to cancel; HELP for help.

Opt out

Users can reply STOP to SMS messages, reply HELP for help, email admin@wbwsupport.com, or delete their account in Settings.
