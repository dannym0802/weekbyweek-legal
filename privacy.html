Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Danny Mauro (“we”, “us”), operating the mobile application Week by Week, collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app and related SMS services.

Information we collect

  • Phone number — used for account sign-in via one-time SMS codes.
  • First name — shown in your profile inside the app.
  • App content you create — habit names, categories, and weekly checkmarks, stored so they sync across your devices.
  • Technical data — limited authentication and delivery metadata needed to operate sign-in (for example, SMS delivery via our providers).

How we use information

  • To create and secure your account with SMS one-time passcodes.
  • To provide Week by Week habit tracking and sync your data.
  • To respond to support requests you send us.
  • To comply with law and protect against abuse.

SMS / text messaging

When you enter your phone number in Week by Week and tap Send code, you consent to receive SMS messages for account authentication (one-time passcodes). Message frequency varies based on how often you sign in. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages, or HELP for help. You may also delete your account in Settings.

SMS delivery is provided through Supabase Auth and Twilio acting as our processors solely to send authentication messages.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers needed to run the app (currently Supabase for database/auth and Twilio for SMS), under agreements that limit use to providing those services. We do not sell personal information.

Retention & deletion

We keep account and habit data while your account is active. You can delete your account in the app under Settings, which removes your profile and habit data from our systems (subject to short-term backups and legal requirements).
